# libSceAgcDriver

## change [PS4 (GCN) multisampled color targets]

- [PS4 (GCN) multisampled color targets](../../../core/libs/prx/libSceAgcDriver/Graphics/src/State.cpp) (libSceAgcDriver, PS4) - not checked on a console. A 2, 4 or 8-sample target is host-resident, as on the AGC path, including EQAA (2 or 4 fragments, with COMPRESSION and an FMASK base) and one-fragment targets. FMASK contents are not modeled: it is guarded, never written, over CB_COLOR_FMASK_SLICE tile max + 1 times 64 pixels of 2 bits per sample for 2 fragments, 4 for 4 and 1 bit for 8 samples for one fragment, as in [addrlib](https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa/-/blob/mesa-25.2.8/src/amd/addrlib/src/r800/siaddrlib.cpp#L3211-3301). With FAST_CLEAR or COMPRESSION, a target storing 2 or more fragments takes its CMASK (CMASK_SLICE tile max + 1 times 128 bytes) for fast clears. More fragments than samples, more than 8 samples and a single-sample or one-fragment fast clear throw. A CB metadata pass may cover only the visible rows and columns of a GCN target its tile mode pads. The initial contents of a 2D-thin or 2D-display target, and the footprint of a 2D MSAA T# over a 2D-thin, 2D-display or 2D-depth surface, follow addrlib's macro tiling with each fragment at fragment times 64 pixels in the micro tile (color) or interleaved per pixel (depth), split into slices past the tile split with the bank rotation of each split, as in [egbaddrlib](https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/mesa/mesa/-/blob/mesa-25.2.8/src/amd/addrlib/src/r800/egbaddrlib.cpp); this addressing is not checked on a console.
